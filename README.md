# Pizza.Recipe - # Halal Mixed Pepperoni-Meat Pizza  

## Ingredients
- Halal Pepperoni | 200g | Sliced 
- Chicken Breast | 300g | Diced
- Pizza Dough | 1 ball |
- Tomato Sauce | 1/2 cup |
- Cheese | 1.5 cups | Shredded

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (Preheating).
2. Spread sauce on dough.
3. Add pepperoni and chicken.
4. Top with cheese.
5. Bake for 12-15 mins until crust is golden (Baking).
6. Cool for 2 mins before serving.
